=== MATHEMATICS ===
Q: If 2x + 3 = 7, find x
A: x = 2
Explanation: Subtract 3 from both sides: 2x = 4, then divide by 2: x = 2

Q: What is the derivative of x^2?
A: 2x
Explanation: Using the power rule: bring down the exponent and reduce it by 1

Q: Simplify (x^2 - 4)/(x - 2)
A: x + 2
Explanation: Factor numerator as (x+2)(x-2), cancel (x-2) to get x+2

Q: Find the value of sin(30°)
A: 0.5
Explanation: sin(30°) = 1/2 = 0.5, this is a standard trigonometric value

Q: If log(x) = 2, find x
A: x = 100
Explanation: log base 10 of x = 2 means x = 10^2 = 100

=== PHYSICS ===
Q: What is Newton's Second Law of Motion?
A: Force equals mass times acceleration (F = ma)
Explanation: The acceleration of an object depends on the net force acting on it and its mass

Q: What is the unit of electric current?
A: Ampere (A)
Explanation: Electric current is measured in Amperes, named after Andre-Marie Ampere

Q: Calculate the speed of an object that travels 100m in 5 seconds
A: 20 m/s
Explanation: Speed = Distance/Time = 100/5 = 20 m/s

Q: What is the law of conservation of energy?
A: Energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed
Explanation: The total energy in a closed system remains constant

Q: What is the frequency of a wave with period 0.02 seconds?
A: 50 Hz
Explanation: Frequency = 1/Period = 1/0.02 = 50 Hz

=== CHEMISTRY ===
Q: What is the atomic number of Carbon?
A: 6
Explanation: Carbon has 6 protons in its nucleus, giving it atomic number 6

Q: What is the chemical formula for water?
A: H2O
Explanation: Water consists of 2 hydrogen atoms bonded to 1 oxygen atom

Q: Balance this equation: H2 + O2 → H2O
A: 2H2 + O2 → 2H2O
Explanation: We need 4 hydrogen and 2 oxygen on each side to balance

Q: What type of bond is formed between Na and Cl?
A: Ionic bond
Explanation: Sodium transfers an electron to Chlorine, creating oppositely charged ions that attract

Q: What is the pH of a neutral solution?
A: 7
Explanation: pH 7 is neutral, below 7 is acidic, above 7 is basic/alkaline

=== ENGLISH ===
Q: Identify the figure of speech: 'The wind whispered through the trees'
A: Personification
Explanation: Giving human qualities (whispering) to a non-human thing (wind)

Q: What is a synonym for 'eloquent'?
A: Articulate, fluent, expressive
Explanation: Eloquent means well-spoken and persuasive in language

Q: Identify the grammatical error: 'Neither John nor his friends was present'
A: 'was' should be 'were'
Explanation: With 'neither...nor', the verb agrees with the noun closest to it (friends = plural = were)

Q: What is the plural of 'curriculum'?
A: Curricula
Explanation: Curriculum is a Latin word, its plural follows Latin rules: curricula

=== BIOLOGY ===
Q: What is the powerhouse of the cell?
A: Mitochondria
Explanation: Mitochondria produce ATP through cellular respiration, providing energy for cell activities

Q: What is osmosis?
A: Movement of water from low concentration to high concentration through a semi-permeable membrane
Explanation: Water moves to dilute the more concentrated solution until equilibrium is reached

Q: What is the function of the nucleus in a cell?
A: Controls cell activities and contains genetic material (DNA)
Explanation: The nucleus acts as the control center, directing protein synthesis and cell reproduction

Q: Name the four bases found in DNA
A: Adenine, Thymine, Guanine, Cytosine
Explanation: A pairs with T, G pairs with C in the double helix structure
